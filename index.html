By using our services, you agree that your personal, behavioral, and genomic information may be used for internal research, product development, and algorithm testing.

We may share data with research partners, business partners, and affiliated entities for data analysis, model training, and scientific collaboration.

Information may be retained for as long as necessary to operate the services, comply with legal obligations, or support ongoing research initiatives.

In the event of a corporate transaction, merger, bankruptcy, or similar event, user data may be transferred to a successor organization, including data that is not separately identifiable.

We may combine information from your account, surveys, health questionnaires, location signals, and third-party sources to improve products and research outcomes.

Deletion requests may be limited by legal requirements, research constraints, or the operational complexity of removing data from aggregated or derived systems.
